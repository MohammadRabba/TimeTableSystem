   691→   691→# 18. HARD CONSTRAINTS
   692→   692→
   693→   693→The following must never be violated.
   694→   694→
   695→   695→## Teacher conflict
   696→   696→
   697→   697→One teacher cannot teach two lessons simultaneously.
   698→   698→
   699→   699→## Class conflict
   700→   700→
   701→   701→One class cannot have two lessons simultaneously.
   702→   702→
   703→   703→## Room conflict
   704→   704→
   705→   705→One room cannot be used by two lessons simultaneously.
   706→   706→
   707→   707→## Teacher availability
   708→   708→
   709→   709→Do not schedule a teacher in forbidden slots.
   710→   710→
   711→   711→## Teacher day off
   712→   712→
   713→   713→Do not schedule teaching or duties on a teacher's day off unless explicitly overridden.
   714→   714→
   715→   715→## Required weekly lessons
   716→   716→
   717→   717→All required lessons must be scheduled.
   718→   718→
   719→   719→## Room compatibility
   720→   720→
   721→   721→Laboratory lessons must use compatible rooms.
   722→   722→
   723→   723→## Capacity
   724→   724→
   725→   725→Room capacity must support the class.
   726→   726→
   727→   727→## Duty conflict
   728→   728→
   729→   729→A teacher cannot teach while assigned to a duty.
   730→   730→
   731→   731→## Fixed lessons
   732→   732→
   733→   733→A fixed lesson cannot be moved automatically unless the administrator explicitly allows it.

   738→   738→
   739→   739→Optimize:

   745→   745→5. Avoid unnecessary consecutive lessons.
   746→   746→6. Avoid too many lessons in one day.
   747→   747→7. Respect teacher preferences.
   748→   748→8. Respect preferred periods.
   749→   749→9. Avoid unwanted first periods.
   750→   750→10. Avoid unwanted last periods.
   751→   751→11. Avoid repeated same subject in one day.
   752→   752→12. Spread lessons throughout the week.
   753→   753→13. Reduce idle gaps.
   754→   754→14. Improve overall timetable quality.

   788→   788→# 21. SCHEDULING ALGORITHM
   789→   789→
   790→   790→Use OR-Tools CP-SAT.
   791→   791→
   792→   792→Represent each lesson occurrence as a scheduling variable.
   793→   793→
   794→   794→Conceptually:
   795→   795→
   796→   796→```text
   797→   797→X(lesson, day, period, room)
   798→   798→```
   799→   799→
   800→   800→where:
   801→   801→
   802→   802→```text
   803→   803→X = 1
   804→   804→```
   805→   805→
   806→   806→means the lesson occupies that slot.
   807→   807→
   808→   808→Add constraints for:
   809→   809→
   810→   810→* teacher
   811→   811→* class
   812→   812→* room
   813→   813→* availability
   814→   814→* workload
   815→   815→* duties
   816→   816→* rooms
   817→   817→* fixed lessons
   818→   818→* consecutive lessons
   819→   819→* weekly occurrence count
   820→   820→
   821→   821→Then create an optimization objective.
   822→   822→
   823→   823→Example:
   824→   824→
   825→   825→```text
   826→   826→Minimize(
   827→   827→    teacher_gaps * weight
   828→   828→    +
   829→   829→    subject_clustering * weight
   830→   830→    +
   831→   831→    seventh_period_deviation * weight
   832→   832→    +
   833→   833→    unwanted_slots * weight
   834→   834→)
   835→   835→```
   836→   836→
