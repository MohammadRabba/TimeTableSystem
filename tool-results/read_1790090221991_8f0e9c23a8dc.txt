   691→# 18. HARD CONSTRAINTS
   692→
   693→The following must never be violated.
   694→
   695→## Teacher conflict
   696→
   697→One teacher cannot teach two lessons simultaneously.
   698→
   699→## Class conflict
   700→
   701→One class cannot have two lessons simultaneously.
   702→
   703→## Room conflict
   704→
   705→One room cannot be used by two lessons simultaneously.
   706→
   707→## Teacher availability
   708→
   709→Do not schedule a teacher in forbidden slots.
   710→
   711→## Teacher day off
   712→
   713→Do not schedule teaching or duties on a teacher's day off unless explicitly overridden.
   714→
   715→## Required weekly lessons
   716→
   717→All required lessons must be scheduled.
   718→
   719→## Room compatibility
   720→
   721→Laboratory lessons must use compatible rooms.
   722→
   723→## Capacity
   724→
   725→Room capacity must support the class.
   726→
   727→## Duty conflict
   728→
   729→A teacher cannot teach while assigned to a duty.
   730→
   731→## Fixed lessons
   732→
   733→A fixed lesson cannot be moved automatically unless the administrator explicitly allows it.
